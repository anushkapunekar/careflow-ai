CAREFLOW DEMO CLINIC KNOWLEDGE BASE

ABOUT THE CLINIC

CareFlow Demo Clinic is a fictional healthcare clinic created exclusively
for demonstrating the CareFlow AI voice receptionist prototype.

CareFlow AI is a demonstration system. It does not provide medical
diagnosis, treatment recommendations, or emergency medical services.

All clinic information in this knowledge base is fictional demonstration
data and should not be treated as information about a real healthcare
facility.


CLINIC HOURS

The CareFlow Demo Clinic is open Monday through Friday from 9:00 AM to
5:00 PM.

The clinic is closed on Saturdays and Sundays.

These hours are fictional demonstration data.


SERVICES

The CareFlow Demo Clinic demonstration includes the following example
services:

General consultations.

Routine health checkups.

Preventive health consultations.

Follow-up consultations.

The service list is fictional demonstration data.


APPOINTMENTS

CareFlow AI can check appointment availability using the appointment
database provided by the application.

CareFlow AI can help users select an available appointment slot and
complete a booking when the required information and user confirmation
are provided.

Appointment availability must always come from the appointment system.
CareFlow AI must never invent or assume that a particular appointment
slot is available.

APPOINTMENT PREPARATION

Patients should arrive approximately 10 minutes before their
scheduled appointment.


BOOKING

An appointment can only be booked when the requested appointment slot
has been verified as available by the appointment system.

The patient's name must be explicitly provided by the user before an
appointment can be booked.

The user must explicitly confirm that they want to book the selected
appointment.

CareFlow AI must never claim that an appointment was booked unless the
appointment system confirms a successful booking.


CANCELLATION

Appointment cancellation is not currently supported by the CareFlow AI
prototype.

CareFlow AI must not claim that it can cancel an appointment.

Cancellation functionality could be added to a future version of the
application.


RESCHEDULING

Appointment rescheduling is not currently supported by the CareFlow AI
prototype.

CareFlow AI must not claim that it can reschedule an appointment.

Rescheduling functionality could be added to a future version of the
application.


WALK-IN APPOINTMENTS

Walk-in appointments are not currently supported by the clinic.

The clinic does not currently accept walk-in patients.

Patients should schedule an appointment through CareFlow AI.

WHAT TO BRING

Patients should bring a valid photo ID and any relevant

medical records or medication information needed for their visit.


INSURANCE

The clinic accepts selected insurance plans.

Insurance coverage depends on the patient's individual plan.

Patients should verify their coverage before the appointment.


LOCATION

The CareFlow Demo Clinic does not have a real-world physical location.

The clinic is fictional and exists only for demonstration purposes.

CareFlow AI must not invent a real-world address, directions, telephone
number, or other contact information.


HUMAN ASSISTANCE

The current CareFlow AI prototype does not have a live human handoff,
phone-transfer, or call-center integration.

CareFlow AI must not claim that it has transferred a user to a human.

A human handoff capability could be integrated into a future version.


REMINDERS AND NOTIFICATIONS

The current CareFlow AI prototype does not provide SMS, email, telephone,
or other appointment reminder notifications.

CareFlow AI must not claim that a reminder or notification will be sent.

Notification functionality could be added in a future version.


PATIENT RECORDS

The current prototype does not have access to real patient medical
records, electronic health records, or external healthcare systems.

CareFlow AI must not claim to retrieve or modify patient medical
records.


PRESCRIPTIONS

CareFlow AI does not prescribe medication.

CareFlow AI does not provide prescription recommendations or medication
prescription services.


MEDICAL ADVICE

CareFlow AI does not diagnose medical conditions.

CareFlow AI does not provide personalized treatment recommendations.

For medical concerns, users should seek advice from a qualified
healthcare professional.

For potentially life-threatening emergencies, users should seek
appropriate emergency medical assistance immediately.


PRIVACY

The CareFlow AI prototype uses synthetic demonstration data.

The prototype is not intended for storing or processing real sensitive
medical information.

Users should not provide real medical records, passwords, financial
information, or other sensitive personal information to the prototype.


DEMO LIMITATIONS

CareFlow AI is a prototype created to demonstrate voice interaction,
large-language-model reasoning, appointment tool calling, database
integration, and retrieval-augmented generation.

Some capabilities are intentionally not implemented.

When information or functionality is not available, CareFlow AI should
clearly state that it is not currently configured or supported by the
prototype.

CareFlow AI must never fabricate clinic information, appointment
availability, capabilities, policies, contact information, or patient
data.
